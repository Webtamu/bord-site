Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

The short version

  • We collect what it takes to seat you at a real table with people who show up: your phone number, a handle and table name, your date of birth, and the nights you join.
  • We never sell your information, never use it for advertising, and don't put ad or tracking tools in the app.
  • Your location is used to confirm you're in a bord city and to point you to your table. We don't keep a history of where you go.
  • Other players see your table name, handle, piece and standing. Not your phone number, surname, birthday or photo.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and everything attached to it at any time.

1. Who we are

bord ("bord", "we", "us") makes the bord app for iPhone and Android and runs bordgame.app. bord gets you from "bored at home" to a seat at a board-game table at a partner shop near you, with other players. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the bord apps, bordgame.app and our invite links, and it is part of our Terms and Conditions.

bord is based in San Diego, California. Questions go to privacy@bordgame.app.

Information from using bord

  • Nights and tables: the nights you join or cancel, the tables you're seated at, whether you checked in, drink orders, and the games you play.
  • Standing: your reliability standing (New, Reliable, Trusted or Probation), which comes from showing up, late cancellations and no-shows.
  • After a game: who you'd play with again, and any reports you make. Other players can't see what you chose.
  • Friends: friend requests, friends and blocks.
  • Invites: invites you send and whether invited friends joined and played.
  • Membership: if you subscribe, your plan and its status (trial, active, renewal and cancellation dates).

Information from your device, with your permission

  • Location (precise and approximate), only while you're using the app. Used to check that you're in a city bord serves, to show nights near you, and to confirm you've arrived at your table. If you ask us to bring bord to your city, we save that city and the rough area you asked from. We don't keep a history of your location. You can say no; parts of the app that need it will ask again.
  • Contacts (optional). If you choose to find friends from your contacts, the phone numbers are scrambled on your phone (a one-way hash) before anything is sent. We compare the scrambled values to find friends already on bord. Your contacts themselves never leave your phone, and we don't store the list.
  • Photos (optional). Photos you add stay on your phone unless you choose to share them, for example with the players from your table.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, we send reminders about nights you've joined.

Invite links

When someone opens a bord invite link on bordgame.app, we may count the visit: the invite code, where the link was shared from if it says, and the device type (iPhone, Android or other). We don't record names or IP addresses in that count.

What we don't collect

We don't use advertising identifiers, ad networks, or third-party analytics or tracking tools in the app. We don't collect your card details: payments for memberships go through the App Store or Google Play, and drink orders through our payment provider, which sends us confirmation of the payment, not your card.

3. How we use information

  • To run bord: create your account, show nights near you, match and seat players, tell everyone where and when, and handle check-in and drinks.
  • To keep tables good: track reliability, act on reports, and pause accounts that make nights worse for people.
  • To keep bord secure, prevent fraud and duplicate accounts, and enforce our terms.
  • To credit invites, run memberships, and contact you about your account, nights you've joined, and changes to bord.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how bord is doing (for example, how many people come back) so we can make it better.
  • To comply with the law.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.

4. Who sees what

Other players

Players at your table and your friends see your table name, handle, piece and standing tier, and who's coming to a night you've joined. They don't see your phone number, surname, date of birth, location or your post-game choices.

Partner shops

A shop hosting a night sees who's coming and checking in (table names), drinks ordered, and counts for its nights. Shops don't get your phone number or date of birth.

Service providers

We use a small number of companies to run bord. They process information only on our behalf and under contract:

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in, on Amazon Web Services servers in Canada.
  • An SMS provider (through Supabase) delivers your sign-in codes.
  • Apple and Google distribute the app, process membership payments, and deliver notifications.
  • Our payment provider processes drink orders.
  • Cloudflare routes our email and protects our internal tools.
  • GitHub hosts bordgame.app.
  • OpenFreeMap serves the map tiles in the app. Your phone requests tiles directly, like any map; we don't send it anything about you.

Legal and safety

We may share information if the law requires it, or if we believe in good faith it's needed to protect someone's safety, investigate abuse, or defend bord's rights. If bord is ever merged or acquired, your information would move with it, under this policy.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile and everything attached to it (nights, standing, friends, invites, pieces) within 30 days. We may keep a minimum for longer where we must: records the law requires, payment records held by Apple, Google or our payment provider, and reports about safety, so a removed account can't simply return. Totals we've already calculated, like "40 people played this week", contain no personal information and stay.

6. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: your handle, table name and piece are in the app under You. For anything else, write to us.
  • Delete your account: email privacy@bordgame.app with the subject "Delete my bord account" and the phone number and handle on your account. You don't need the app to do this. We'll confirm it's you and delete your account and its data within 30 days.
  • Permissions: turn location, contacts, photos and notifications on or off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Get a copy: ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you.

California residents

If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to request a copy, to correct it, and to delete it, without being treated differently for asking. We don't sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we don't use sensitive information beyond what's needed to run bord. To make a request, email privacy@bordgame.app; we may need to confirm it's you, and you can have an authorized agent ask for you.

Outside the United States

bord is currently for players in the cities we serve in the United States. If you use it from elsewhere, your information is processed in the United States and Canada, and you may have additional rights under local law, which we'll honor.

7. Security

Information travels over encrypted connections, passwords are stored only in hashed form, and access to the database is limited by row-level security, so the app can only read what it's meant to. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

8. Children

bord is only for people 18 and older. We ask for your date of birth at sign-up and don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and let you know in the app before the change applies to you.

10. Contact us

Email privacy@bordgame.app. We're a small team in San Diego, and a person reads every message.
