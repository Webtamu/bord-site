Delete your bord data

You can ask us to delete some or all of the data in your bord account and keep the account itself. To remove the account too, see Delete your bord account.

How to request it

  1. Tap the button below. It opens an email to privacy@bordgame.app with the subject "Delete my bord data".
  2. Add your bord handle (your @name) and the phone number on the account, and keep the lines for the data you want deleted (or say "all of it").
  3. We'll reply to confirm it's you, then delete it within 30 days and email you when it's done.
Email us to delete my data

Or write to privacy@bordgame.app from any email address.

What you can have deleted

  • Your history: nights joined, tables, check-ins and drink orders.
  • Friends and blocks.
  • Invites you sent, and the invite code you used.
  • Cities you asked bord to come to, and the area you asked from.
  • Photos and anything else you shared in bord.
  • Your post-game choices (who you'd play with again).

What stays while you keep the account

  • What the account needs to work: your phone number, handle, table name, date of birth (bord is 18+), password and piece. These go only when you delete the account.
  • Your standing (New, Reliable, Trusted or Probation), since it keeps tables fair for everyone, plus any safety reports about the account.
  • Payment records held by Apple, Google or our payment provider for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Totals such as "40 people played this week", which contain no personal information.

Everything you ask us to remove is gone within 30 days. Our Privacy Policy has the details.
