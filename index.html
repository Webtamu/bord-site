How it works

Queue. Match. Walk over.

Queue

Queue up or book a session

Pick a shop on the map and queue up, or book a scheduled session. Bring a friend — duos queue together, or send an invite link.

Match

Get matched to a table

We seat you with other verified players — everyone is 18+ and phone-verified. Your table shows the location and game time.

Play

Check in and play

Show your voucher for a free drink, check in at the table, and play. When the game ends, log who won — winners go on the shop’s leaderboard.

Trust

Real people, real tables.

A game only works if everyone shows up. Bord is built so you know who you’re sitting down with.

Verified players

Everyone verifies their phone number and is 18 or older. Games happen in public, at staffed boba shops.

Trust score

Show up and play, and your trust score climbs — higher scores match first.

New → Reliable → Trusted

Play with friends

Add a friend, queue as a duo, or send an invite link so they can join your session. Duos are never split up.

For shops

Full tables, prepaid drinks.

Bord brings players to your shop and pays for their drinks before they walk in.

  • ◆ Prepaid drink vouchers. We buy voucher blocks from you up front. A player shows their voucher, you hand them a drink — it’s already paid for.
  • ◆ Vendor portal. Sign in with your vendor account to see how many customers Bord brought in, vouchers redeemed, and upcoming sessions.
  • ◆ You set the schedule. Choose which nights and tables Bord can book — sessions fill your slow hours.
  • ◆ Your own leaderboard. Every shop has one. Winners sign their name — and come back to defend it.
