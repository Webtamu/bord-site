For shops

Full tables, good vibes.

Bord brings new customers through your door.

  • ◆ We provide the games. Bord supplies a board game that lives at your shop. Nothing to buy or manage.
  • ◆ People through the door. Every seat we fill is a customer at your counter.
  • ◆ You set the schedule. Choose which nights and tables Bord can book — sessions fill your slow hours.
  • ◆ Your own leaderboard. Every shop has one. Winners sign their name — and come back to defend it.
