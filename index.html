How it works

Pick a night. Get dealt in. Stamp it.

Pick a night

A shop, a night, a seat

The map shows every boba shop running a night this week — the die on each pin is how many. Tap one, take a seat. Bring a friend; duos are never split.

PN HZ

Get dealt in

Your table fills up

Three other verified players get dealt into the chairs around yours. You see who’s coming; they see you. Nobody walks in cold.

Stamp it

Show up, get the stamp

Check in at the table and the night goes on your card. Take the one photo; it lands in your album, and on the shop’s page if you say so.

Trust

Real people, real tables.

Showing up is the whole game. Everything here is built to make it the norm.

Verified

Everyone is 18+, phone-verified, and ID-checked. Nights happen in public, at staffed shops, at a table with your name on it.

Karma

Show up and your standing climbs. It’s a word the table sees, never a number.

New → Reliable → Trusted

Friends

bring someone — duos are seated together, always. or send the link and let a friend take the open chair.

For shops

Your slow nights, full.

You keep the games you already have. We bring the people.

  • Your shelf, your rules. Whatever’s on the shelf is what gets played. Nothing to buy, nothing to manage.
  • People through the door. Every seat we fill is a customer at your counter, on a night that was quiet.
  • You set the nights. A standing week — the tables and evenings bord can fill. Staff take zero taps.
  • Your shop’s page. Every night people share lands on it: the photos, the nights, the regulars.
