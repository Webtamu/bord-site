how it works

pick a night. get taped in. keep the page.

pick a night

a shop, a night, a chair

the map is pinned with every boba shop running a night this week. tap a pin, take a chair. bring a friend — duos are never split.

get taped in

your page fills up

three other verified players land on tonight’s page beside you. you see who’s coming; they see you. nobody walks in cold.

keep the page

show up, get the star

check in at the table and the night goes in your album with a gold star on it. take the one photo; it lands on the page, and on the shop’s if you say so.

trust

real people, real tables.

showing up is the whole game. the album is built to make it the norm.

verified

everyone is 18+, phone-verified, and ID-checked. nights happen in public, at staffed shops, at a table with your name on it.

the album is the karma

trusted: · 12 nights kept, 0 missed

every night you keep gets a photo. flake, and the page stays empty — everyone sees the gap.

friends

bring someone — duos are seated together, always. or send the link and let a friend take the open chair.

for shops

your slow nights, full.

you keep the games you already have. we bring the people.

  • your shelf, your rules. whatever’s on the shelf is what gets played. nothing to buy, nothing to manage.
  • people through the door. every chair we fill is a customer at your counter, on a night that was quiet.
  • you set the nights. a standing week — the tables and evenings bord can fill. staff take zero taps.
  • your shop’s page. every night people share lands in your album: the photos, the nights, the regulars.
