How it works

Pick a night. Get dealt in. Stamp it.

Pick a night

A shop, a night, a seat

The map shows every boba shop running a night this week. The number on the pin is how many. Tap one, take a seat. Bring a friend if you want, you’ll be seated together.

Get dealt in

Your table fills up

Three other verified players get dealt into the chairs around yours. You see who’s coming and they see you. Nobody walks in cold.

Stamp it

Show up, get the stamp

Check in at the table and the night goes on your card. Take the one photo. It lands in your album, and on the shop’s page if you say so.

The album

The app is a scrapbook. You fill it.

Every night you keep is a stamp on your card and a print on the page. The less you’re in the app, the better it’s working. You’re at a table.

Trust

Real people, real tables.

Showing up is the whole game. Everything here is built to make it the norm.

Verified

Everyone is 18+, phone-verified, and ID verified. Nights happen in public, at staffed shops, at a table with your name on it.

Karma

Show up and your standing climbs. The table sees a word, never a number.

New → Reliable → Trusted

For shops

Your slow nights, full.

You keep the games you already have. We bring the people.

  • Your shelf, your rules. Whatever’s on the shelf is what gets played. Nothing to buy or manage.
  • People through the door. Every seat we fill is someone at your counter on a night that used to be quiet.
  • You set the nights. Tell us which tables and evenings are open each week. Your staff never touch the app.
  • Your shop’s page. The photos people share, the nights, the regulars. All on one page.
