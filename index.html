How it works

Queue. Match. Walk over.

Queue

Match

Walk over

Why people show up

Showing up is the whole game.

A pickup game only works if all four chairs fill. Everything in BORD is built to make showing up the norm.

Verified, every one

Everyone is ID-verified before their first night, and you never meet anyone outside a staffed public shop. Women-only tables are an opt-in filter.

Karma you can see

Reliability is worn, not scored. Show up on time and your tier climbs — and higher tiers match first.

New → Reliable → Trusted

A drink on the line

Your drink is ordered the moment you queue. Skip the night and it goes to the next player — and your karma dips. Cancel before you’re matched and we cancel the drink order, full refund.

For shops

Your quietest tables, working.

BORD fills weeknight dead hours with prepaid players who order, play two hours, and reset the table themselves.

  • ◆ Money moves before the night. We buy drink voucher blocks up front, on a plain invoice. If we ever disappear, unused vouchers stay yours.
  • ◆ Staff take zero taps. Players redeem on their own phones at the counter — a green screen with a moving clock means the drink is already paid. Training is that one sentence.
  • ◆ Players do the physical ops. The highest-karma player sets up the game and packs it away. We supply the crate: three copies of Catan, table tents, one shelf.
  • ◆ The app is the bad guy. It reserves the table, ends the session on time, and says no to flakers — your staff never have to.
