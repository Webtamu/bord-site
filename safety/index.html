Child safety standards

bord, the app for board-game nights at partner shops, has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These are our standards, and how we enforce them. Last updated October 27, 2026.

bord is for adults only

  • Everyone on bord must be 18 or older. We ask for date of birth at sign-up and refuse anyone under 18.
  • bord nights happen in public partner shops, never in private homes.
  • If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it and delete its data.

What is prohibited

On bord, and at any bord night, it is prohibited to:

  • create, share, request or link to child sexual abuse material of any kind, including drawn, edited or AI-generated material;
  • sexualize minors in any way, in photos, captions, messages, handles or table names;
  • groom, solicit or arrange to meet a minor, or use bord to make contact with a minor;
  • use bord to traffic, exploit or endanger a child in any way.

How to report a concern

  1. In the app: after every table you can report a player you sat with. Reports go to the bord team, not to the player.
  2. Any time: email safety@bordgame.app with what happened and the handle, shop or night involved. You don't need an account or the app.
  3. If a child is in danger right now, call 911. You can also report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children at CyberTipline.org or 1-800-843-5678.

What we do when something is reported

  • A person on the bord team reviews every child safety report, as a priority.
  • We remove the content and permanently ban the account, and we take steps so it can't simply sign up again.
  • We report apparent CSAM and child exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as US law requires, and cooperate with law enforcement. We keep the relevant records for them as the law requires, rather than deleting them.
  • We tell the partner shop involved when a concern relates to a night at their shop.

Compliance

bord complies with applicable child safety laws, including the US federal reporting requirements for providers (18 U.S.C. § 2258A), and with Google Play's and Apple's child safety policies. These standards are part of our Privacy Policy and our Terms and Conditions, which every player agrees to at sign-up.

Contact

Our child safety point of contact can be reached at safety@bordgame.app, and can speak to how bord prevents CSAM and responds to reports.
