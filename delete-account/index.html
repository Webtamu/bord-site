Delete your bord account

You can ask us to delete your bord account and the data attached to it at any time. You don't need the app to do it.

How to request deletion

  1. Tap the button below. It opens an email to privacy@bordgame.app with the subject "Delete my bord account".
  2. Add your bord handle (your @name) and the phone number on the account, then send it.
  3. We'll reply to confirm it's you, then delete your account within 30 days and email you when it's done.
Email us to delete my account

Or write to privacy@bordgame.app from any email address.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: phone number, handle, table name, date of birth, password and your piece.
  • Your history: nights joined, tables, check-ins, drink orders and your standing.
  • Friends, blocks, invites you sent and the invite code you used.
  • Anything you shared in bord, and your post-game choices.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Payment records for memberships and drink orders, held by Apple, Google or our payment provider for as long as tax and accounting law requires. They're no longer linked to a bord profile.
  • Safety reports about the account, kept so a removed account can't simply come back.
  • Totals such as "40 people played this week", which contain no personal information.

Everything else is gone within 30 days of your request. Our Privacy Policy has the details.
